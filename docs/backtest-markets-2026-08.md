## What it shows

1. **The approach generalizes to every market.** Persistence captures
   75–96% and climatology 85–99% of the perfect-hindsight spread across
   Spain, Germany, Italy, Portugal, and ERCOT — five markets, three data
   sources, two continents. The "it travels" claim the live ledgers hint at
   is confirmed historically over ~7 months.
2. **Climatology beats persistence in EVERY market** (ES 95.8 vs 93.2, DE
   94.2 vs 88.4, IT 84.9 vs 74.6, PT 95.2 vs 92.7, ERCOT 98.9 vs 96.1). The
   Spanish finding is a UNIVERSAL regularity, not Spain-specific — and
   rank-blend tracks climatology closely everywhere. The weekly-naive is the
   weakest baseline in all four European markets.
3. **Markets differ sharply in difficulty.** Italy (IT-SUD) is the hard
   one: 74.6% persistence capture, 18 losing days, tau 0.60 — the least
   persistent, most volatile zone, and therefore the market where a better
   forecast (v2) would add the MOST value. Spain and Portugal are easiest
   (~93–96%, ~0 losses); Germany sits between (persistence 88%, 8 losses).
4. **ERCOT caveat**: 96–99% capture but only 30 days AND day-ahead only —
   ERCOT's real volatility is in real-time, which this does not touch. Treat
   the ERCOT numbers as provisional.

## Bearing on decisions

- **v2 gate**: Italy is the strongest case for a forecasting model — the
  largest persistence-to-oracle gap and the most losing days. If v2 is
  built, IT (and DE) is where it earns its keep; ES/PT are near-saturated by
  the dumb baselines.
- **Monetization**: "85–96% capture across 5 markets, climatology beats
  persistence universally" is a defensible, quotable cross-market claim —
  and it names Italy as the market where our forecast would be most sellable.
